Export to Skybrush (.skyc)

      
Downloads the formation on screen as a Skybrush
      show file — a takeoff from a ground grid, the held shape, and a
      landing back to the grid — ready to load in Skybrush Viewer or
      Live. The drones are spaced by the setting below (the picture's
      width follows from it); the takeoff grid has its own spacing,
      below, so the ground crew is not forced to match the picture's
      spread. The shape is centred on the altitude.
