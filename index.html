Why drones

The finale, without the fireworks problems

Drone light show

  • Custom artwork — your initials, logo or date, animated
  • No fire, no sparks: flies where burn bans and venues say no to fireworks
  • Quiet — kind to horses, dogs and neighbors in horse country
  • No debris to rain on guests, vineyards or waterways
  • Reusable and repeatable across a whole weekend

Traditional fireworks

  • Beautiful, but the same bursts every time — nothing personal
  • Fire risk, permits and venue restrictions in dry months
  • Loud enough to spook animals for miles
  • Fallout over lawns, vines, cars and water
  • One shot, one night — weather can't be rebooked easily

Already booked fireworks? A drone show pairs beautifully alongside them — or carries the nights fireworks can't fly.

Questions

Before you ask

How much does a show cost?

It depends on the package, how custom the artwork is, and your venue — so we quote every event individually rather than posting a one-size price. Send the inquiry below with your date and a rough budget, and we'll tell you honestly what that budget can put in the sky.

How far ahead do we need to book?

As early as you can. Design, approvals and venue coordination take weeks, and the FAA authorization process needs lead time — for weddings and fall alumni weekends, think months, not weeks. Our 2027 founding-season interest list is open now. Planning a 200+ drone finale? Tell us as early as you can — the additional units take extra lead time to secure.

What does our venue need?

An open launch area roughly the size of a tennis court, a clear view of the sky above it, and the venue's permission. Estates, vineyards, farms and fairgrounds across Central Virginia are ideal. We'll visit or review the site with you before confirming.

Is it safe and legal?

Drone light shows are regulated for good reason, and every show is planned around FAA rules: FAA Part 107 licensed pilots, the airspace authorizations your venue needs, a dedicated safety observer, insured operations, and a defined flight zone that keeps the fleet away from your guests. If conditions aren't safe, the show doesn't fly — simple as that.

What happens if the weather turns?

Light rain and wind limits are part of the plan: we monitor the forecast with you in the days before, and your contract covers a weather backup plan so a storm doesn't take your finale with it.

Where do you fly?

Charlottesville and Central Virginia are home — Albemarle, the Blue Ridge foothills, and the vineyard country around them. Further afield in Virginia? Ask in the inquiry; founding-season travel is on the table.

Can it be a surprise?

Absolutely — proposals and gifts are our favorite kind of secret. Tell us it's a surprise in the inquiry and we'll keep every conversation discreet.
